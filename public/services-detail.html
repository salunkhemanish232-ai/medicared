Emergency care

Fast clinical guidance for urgent health concerns.

Emergency care through Medicare supports patient triage and guidance toward the right next step. We do not provide emergency medical treatment or emergency room services.

For real emergencies, contact the appropriate local emergency service provider immediately. Medicare can help route and coordinate non-urgent follow-up care.
