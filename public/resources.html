Common questions

Good care starts with clear answers.

How early should I arrive?

Arrive 15 minutes before an in-person appointment so the team can confirm your details comfortably.

Can I change my appointment?

Use the support chat or contact the Medicare team as soon as possible so we can help find another time.

What if I need urgent help?

For an emergency, call your local emergency number immediately. Medicare support is available for non-emergency guidance.

Always important

For urgent symptoms

Severe chest pain, trouble breathing, sudden weakness, or heavy bleeding needs immediate emergency care.
