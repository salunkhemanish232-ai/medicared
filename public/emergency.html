Emergency care

For urgent medical concerns, seek immediate local emergency care.

Medicare provides non-emergency support and direction. We do not provide emergency medical services or emergency room treatment.

Location & access

Patients should use a licensed local emergency department or contact the most appropriate local emergency service provider for immediate care.

Contact support

For non-urgent questions, contact the hospital support team through our general contact page.

What we can do

We can help guide patients toward the right department, available appointment options, and safe next steps.
